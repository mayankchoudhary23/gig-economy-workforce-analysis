# 🗺️ Mapping the Invisible Workforce: U.S. Gig Economy Analysis

> An interactive, narrative-driven data visualization project analyzing the geographic, economic, and demographic structure of the U.S. gig economy.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Built a multi-dashboard Tableau story using county-level data from three major sources to examine:
- Where gig work is geographically concentrated
- How gig earnings compare to local cost-of-living benchmarks
- Who participates in independent work across income, age, and education groups

---

## ❓ Research Questions

| # | Question |
|---|----------|
| 1 | Where is gig-style independent work most concentrated in the U.S.? |
| 2 | Do gig earnings align with local cost-of-living requirements? |
| 3 | Who participates in gig work, and what are their financial characteristics? |
| 4 | Are there distinct regional types of gig economies? |
| 5 | What do current patterns suggest about the future of gig work? |

---

## 🔍 Key Findings

- **Geographic clustering**: Gig worker density is highly right-skewed — a small number of counties dominate as "gig hotspots"
- **Earnings vs. cost of living**: Higher gig density does not uniformly correspond to higher economic sufficiency
- **Demographics**: Gig work peaks among mid-career age groups
- **Education**: Educational attainment among independent workers rises sharply with income

## 📊 Dashboards & Visualizations

| Dashboard | Focus |
